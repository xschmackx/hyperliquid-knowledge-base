# VOICE AND COVERAGE — Hyperliquid Knowledge Base

Durable doctrine. D3. Recorded as D-HLKB-006.

This page locks who an accepted page is for and what “complete” means. It is not a corpus page and it is not ingest.

Truth, rank, tags, `as-of`, and stale stay in `SOURCE-RANK.md`. This page does not change that order.

---

## Who a page is for

An accepted page must be usable by a stranger in any of these roles, without private context:

- builder
- developer
- user
- client
- regulator
- competitor

One page. One public voice. Do not split the same fact into insider and outsider versions. Taxonomy may later put topics on different shelves. It may not put the same claim behind a private dialect.

A stranger is anyone who has this page and the sources it cites, and does not have this desk's chat, Notebook, or another project's notes.

---

## Stranger voice

Write so the stranger can act on the page without asking this desk what a sentence meant.

| Do | Do not |
|----|--------|
| Use public words for public things. | Use desk slang, packet IDs, or another project's terms as if the reader already knows them. |
| Define a term on first use, or point to the page that defines it. | Assume the reader sat through an earlier chat. |
| Say who did what in plain English. | Write “we” as if the reader is on the inside. |
| State the scoped fact, the source, and the limit of the claim. | Sell, dunk, or coach a trade. |
| Keep the page readable if the only open tab is this page. | Point at Slack, Discord, or chat memory as the explanation. |

Competitor in the audience list means the page must still be usable if the reader is not on Hyperliquid's side. It does not mean write attack copy. Neutral. Not marketing.

Voice does not licence padding. Short and checkable beats long and insider.

---

## What complete means

Completeness is scoped. It is not “everything about Hyperliquid.”

A page is **complete** when all of these hold:

1. The page states its scope in the opening.
2. Every claim inside that scope is either supported under `SOURCE-RANK.md` or marked unknown.
3. Every hole inside that scope is written as a gap, not left silent.
4. Page-level `as-of` is present. The page is not stale, or it is labelled historical.

A page is **not complete** when it:

- omits a fact its own scope promised
- fills a hole with tone, analogy, or “everyone knows”
- looks finished because the gap was deleted

Out of scope is not a completeness failure. Do not drag adjacent topics onto the page to look thorough. Name the adjacent topic only if the stranger would otherwise misread the scope.

---

## A gap is an item, not silence

If Rank 1–3 do not cover a point inside the page's scope:

- Write the gap on the page.
- Do not invent the missing fact.
- Do not drop the heading so the hole disappears.

A written gap is the honest product of this kind. It becomes a Taskmap item only when the Human names that work. Silence is not a gap. Silence is an incomplete page.

---

## Acceptance test (stranger)

Before a page is accepted, it must survive this test:

A stranger in any listed role can read the page once, follow the cited URLs, and tell apart (a) what the official source says, (b) what is secondary or community, and (c) what this desk does not know.

If they need this chat to finish that sentence, the page fails voice.
If they cannot see the hole the scope implied, the page fails coverage.
