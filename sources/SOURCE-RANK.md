# SOURCE RANK — Hyperliquid Knowledge Base

This page is the rule for what may support an accepted corpus page. It is not an index of live URLs.

---

## Rank order

Higher rank wins when sources disagree. A lower-rank source may explain or point. It may not establish a fact that a higher rank contradicts or does not support.

| Rank | Class | What counts |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Official written canon | Documentation and specs published on Hyperliquid-controlled sites and in Hyperliquid-controlled repos that Hyperliquid presents as the written description of the protocol or product. |
| 2 | Official artifacts | First-party code, APIs, release notes, and protocol or product state that Hyperliquid itself publishes or exposes. The artifact is the source. A third-party reading of it is not Rank 2. |
| 3 | Official communication | Announcements from Hyperliquid-controlled accounts and channels that Hyperliquid itself treats as official. Date and URL required. Informal tone does not drop the rank; it limits how far a page may generalise from one message. |
| 4 | Secondary | Independent documentation, research, journalism, dashboards, and mirrors. Tagged. |
| 5 | Community / informal | Unofficial chats, forums, unaffiliated social posts, unsourced threads. Tagged. Never the sole support for an accepted claim. |

**Not a source.** Private context. Rumour. These do not enter an accepted page.

Same rank, later official `as-of` wins for *current* state. Keep an earlier claim only on a page labelled historical.

If Rank 1–3 are silent and lower ranks speak, the accepted page does not treat the lower rank as settled fact.

---

## Tagging (anything not Rank 1–3)

Every citation that is not Rank 1–3 carries an explicit tag next to the claim, not only in a footer a stranger can miss.

| Tag | Use |
|-----|-----|
| `secondary` | Rank 4 |
| `community` | Rank 5 |

The tag sits with URL and `as-of`. Untagged non-official material is not acceptable on an accepted page.

---

## `as-of` + URL on every accepted page

- Every accepted page has a page-level `as-of` date: the date the cited sources were checked.
- Every factual claim that can change has a source URL, or the claim is marked unknown.
- Draft date is not a substitute for check date. If they differ, use the check date.
- Rank 4 and Rank 5 citations still need URL + `as-of` + tag.

A page without page-level `as-of` is not accepted.

---

## Stale rule

A page is **stale** when either:

1. A Rank 1–3 source it cites has changed on the cited point, or
2. The page `as-of` is known to predate a material official change on that point.

Stale pages are not current. Mark them stale. Do not rewrite from memory. Re-check the official source, or record a gap.

Stale is not a licence to erase history. A historical page keeps its old `as-of` and says it is historical.

---

## Unknown stays unknown

If Rank 1–3 do not say it, the corpus does not assert it.

- Do not fill a hole with Rank 4–5 voice or private context.
- "People say" is not a fact.
- A gap is written as a gap. It is not a guessed paragraph.
